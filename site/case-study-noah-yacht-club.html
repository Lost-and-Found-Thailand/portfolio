Case Study

Noah Yacht Club

Yacht Club

Managing full-funnel paid media for Noah Yacht Club since it opened in Phuket, turning its catamaran day trips and private yacht charters into a fully booked schedule two months out.

+3,373% ROAS
The Challenge

A brand-new luxury yacht club needed to build its bookings from zero.

Noah Yacht Club opened in Phuket with catamaran day trips and a 75-foot private charter yacht built for the Andaman Sea, aiming to bring an international standard of maritime hospitality to a market long dominated by budget boat trips. As a brand-new venue with no existing customer base, it needed a paid media system that could build a steady flow of charter and catamaran bookings from day one.

The Strategy

Full-funnel marketing, managed since the club opened.

Three connected workstreams built to turn a brand-new yacht club into a fully booked one.

01

Paid Media

Campaigns built around Noah's catamaran day trips and private yacht charters, targeting Phuket's tourist market and higher-intent charter enquiries.

02

Full-Funnel Marketing

Connecting every stage from first ad view through to a confirmed charter or catamaran booking, not just optimizing for clicks.

03

Conversion Rate Optimization

Ongoing testing of the enquiry and booking flow to keep the schedule filling as demand grew.

The Result

From opening day to Phuket's top yacht club, booked out two months ahead.

Managing Noah Yacht Club's paid media since the day it opened, the campaign has helped it grow into Phuket's top yacht club. Every charter and catamaran slot on the schedule is now booked solid up to two months in advance.
