What I Do

Six connected disciplines — strategy, media, tracking and data — working as one system, rather than six separate vendors.

Paid Media Lead Generation Conversion Tracking Marketing Analytics Conversion Optimization Growth & Digital Strategy
01

Paid Media

Campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn Ads, plus Performance Max and Search — built for efficiency, not just reach. Every campaign is structured around a clear objective, tested creative, and a budget that's allowed to work instead of being guessed at. I've managed $10M+ in ad spend this way, across 100+ brands.

  • Meta & TikTok Ads campaign build & management
  • Google Ads Search, Performance Max & LinkedIn Ads
  • Creative & audience testing frameworks
  • Budget pacing & bid strategy
02

Lead Generation

Lead generation systems designed around qualified enquiries rather than vanity metrics like cost-per-click. That means qualifying traffic before the click, not just sorting through it after the form is submitted.

  • Funnel & offer design
  • Lead qualification logic
  • Landing page-to-CRM handoff
  • Lead scoring frameworks
03

Conversion Tracking

GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, conversion APIs and server-side event tracking — so every dollar of ad spend can be traced back to a real business outcome, not just a pixel fire. This is the foundation everything else depends on.

  • GA4 & Google Tag Manager implementation
  • Meta Conversions API
  • Server-side tracking
  • Cross-platform attribution
04

Marketing Analytics

Measurement systems, dashboards and campaign analysis that turn raw platform data into decisions — what's working, what isn't, and what to do about it next. Reporting built to be read, not just produced.

  • Looker Studio dashboards
  • Multi-channel reporting
  • Attribution modeling
  • Campaign performance analysis
05

Conversion Optimization

Landing pages, forms and funnels rebuilt around how people actually decide, not how a template assumes they will. Changes are proposed, tested and measured — not shipped on instinct.

  • Landing page audits & rebuilds
  • Form & funnel optimization
  • A/B and multivariate testing
  • Page speed & UX improvements
06

Growth & Digital Strategy

Data-driven marketing strategy and growth planning that connects paid, organic and lifecycle channels — SEO, SEM, social and email — into a single plan, not a list of disconnected tactics run in isolation.

  • SEO & SEM strategy
  • Social media & email marketing
  • Channel & budget planning
  • Growth roadmaps
  • Competitive & market analysis
  • Marketing technology audits
